01Engineering Design

Design Intern

Hybrid - Delhi NCR+

Atriksha Engineering Solutions is looking for a Design Intern to support engineering and R&D teams in creating 3D models, product concepts, and manufacturing-ready designs.

Responsibilities

  • Create 3D CAD models and 2D engineering drawings.
  • Assist in DFM for 3D printing, CNC, sheet metal, and molding.
  • Support prototype development, slicing, and print preparation.
  • Conduct research, benchmarking, and documentation.
  • Collaborate on industrial and academic R&D projects.

Requirements

  • Basic engineering drawing and GD&T knowledge.
  • SolidWorks or Fusion 360 preferred.
  • Interest in manufacturing, product design, and prototyping.
  • B.Tech 2nd or 3rd year students in Mechanical, Mechatronics, Production, Industrial Design, or related fields.
