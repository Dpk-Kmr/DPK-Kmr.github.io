Each work was separately searched and its publisher abstract read. Titles, complete author lists in published order, publication dates, and DOI records were checked against publisher metadata and Crossref publisher deposits. Duplicate preprints are excluded.

The original records credit collaborating institutions. They do not establish that the papers were produced at Atriksha or that all coauthors are employees. Preserve all authors with equal visual weight inside the collapsed citation details; preserve equal-contribution notes. The website presents a company research collection and does not publish or promote individual profile links. The 2026 CO₂ paper was published online 12 March, with an issue date of 25 March 2026. ## Content and visuals

Each public summary, three story steps, and limitation are a concise original paraphrase of that paper's abstract and available publisher section snippets. Total source-derived prose is kept below 200 words per paper, including metadata. Original SVG illustrations explain concepts, not recorded experimental results; none reproduces journal figures. Illustrations are labeled “Concept illustration.” They do not generate medical predictions or invent numerical research results.

## Publishing another study

Edit `src/data/research.js`. Add an object to `papers` following an existing record. Verify the exact title, all authors in published order, venue, DOI, dates, and publication status against the publisher. Record equal-contribution notes where applicable. Include a direct `publisherUrl`, stable `doiUrl`, `verifiedDate`, and `sourceNote`. Keep `question`, `approach`, `finding`, `why`, and `limitation` short and grounded in the paper; do not copy its abstract. Add an original concept illustration keyed by its unique `id` in `src/scripts/research.js` (an accessible basic fallback exists). Use a supported `color`: lime, cyan, purple, or amber. Topic filters and publication counts are generated from the catalog. Retain actual author attribution without implying every historical study was conducted at Atriksha. No CMS or server is required: the static data renders directly on GitHub Pages, and local previews do not depend on a fetch request.

Do not add preprints a second time when they describe an already listed journal article. For example, the preprint “Real-Time Model Maintenance Using Kernel-Based Forward Learning Neural Network with Applications to Model Predictive Control” is represented by the published 2024 model-maintenance article.
